# The first loop, run on a real LibreOffice Writer

Private display, bus and runtime directory. Words were typed; each line is a new process on a busy machine, so the times include starting up and connecting.

```
heard: where am I
says:  Untitled 1 — LibreOffice Writer. Nothing is focused.
took:  446 ms from words to answer
heard: what can I do here
says:  Untitled 1 — LibreOffice Writer. 68 controls. File, Edit, View, Insert, Format, Styles, Table, Form, and more.
took:  294 ms from words to answer
heard: press bold
says:  Bold is on.
took:  307 ms from words to answer
heard: press bold
says:  Bold is off.
took:  297 ms from words to answer
heard: press send
says:  Did you mean End? Nothing was pressed.
took:  339 ms from words to answer
heard: press bald
says:  Untitled 1 — LibreOffice Writer shows nothing called bald. Nothing was pressed.
took:  396 ms from words to answer
heard: press redo
says:  Redo is unavailable. Nothing was pressed.
took:  356 ms from words to answer
heard: press paste
says:  Paste is unavailable. Nothing was pressed.
took:  396 ms from words to answer
heard: what is the weather
says:  I heard: what is the weather. Nothing was done.
took:  270 ms from words to answer
```

Speech recognition was run separately: voxtype small.en heard a synthetic "press bold" correctly, taking 2.1 s from a cold start. The half-second target is not met or tested until the recogniser and the loop stay running. "press send" offers End, which is a poor guess and presses nothing. Not run: a live microphone, real Hyprland, or with a blind person.
