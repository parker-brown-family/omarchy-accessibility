"Where am I" now works, because the service follows focus by events. The earlier failure came from the test moving focus by force.

**From a recording to an answer:** 1.1 to 1.2 s for "press bold" spoken by a synthetic voice, of which about 0.8 s was loading the recogniser each time. Transcription alone was about 0.2 s and the answer 0.09 s. With the recogniser kept loaded that adds to about 0.3 s plus the pause after speech. That has not been measured end to end.

**Not run:** a live microphone, `oa-talk`, the plugin inside a running shell, real Hyprland, or anyone blind.
