Reading back only the doubtful words slowly helps people catch recognition mistakes.

> participants missed approximately 50% of recognition errors

> led to a 12% relative increase in participants' ability to detect errors compared to uniformly slowing the audio

**Limits.** The 50% comes from an earlier study of sighted people.
