Blind people learned a building either by exploring it as an audio game or by being directed through it, then walked the real one.

> revealed no significant main effects for condition

> game-based learning strategy was also associated with enhanced performance when participants were required to find alternate routes and short cuts within the target building

**Limits.** A building, not a desktop. Fifteen and fourteen people. The speed difference on the alternate-route task was significant only for the late blind.
