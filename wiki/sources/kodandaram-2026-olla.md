Eight blind people used an agent on their own Windows work for three weeks.

> three-week diary study with 8 blind users using OLLA, a screen-reader-accessible CUA prototype, collecting 1,258 commands across 12 applications

> GPT-5 achieved the highest success rate at 52.5%

> Sometimes I just need it to tell me where I am, what options are available, and what I should do next.

> I want it to ask me before clicking something important. I do not want it making decisions on its own.

**Limits.** Eight people, English, Windows, and models older than today's.
