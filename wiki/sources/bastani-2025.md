Nearly a thousand students; AI raised practice grades and lowered later exam grades, unless it was built to teach.

> having GPT-4 access while solving problems significantly improves performance (48% improvement in grades for GPT Base and 127% for GPT Tutor)

> 17% reduction in grades for GPT Base

> These negative learning effects are largely mitigated by the safeguards in GPT Tutor

**Limits.** Abstract only. Sighted students, one school, mathematics. Read from a copy of the abstract of the working-paper wording.
