1,539 responses on what screen-reader users use.

> 91.3% of respondents report using a screen reader on a mobile device

> Braille usage at 38% is up slightly from 33.3% in 2017

> The sample was not controlled and may not represent all screen reader users

**Limits.** Self-selected. A newer survey was searched for and not found.
